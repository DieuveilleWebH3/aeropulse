# AeroPulse Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AeroPulse should be a professional, welcoming environment for people contributing
code, documentation, reviews, issues, security reports, design ideas, or technical
discussion.

Participants are expected to treat each other with respect regardless of background,
identity, experience level, education, nationality, disability, appearance, race,
ethnicity, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Productive participation includes:

- discussing technical disagreements respectfully;
- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- accepting correction and revising work when evidence changes;
- assuming good faith while evaluating claims critically;
- respecting privacy and confidentiality;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people;
- making room for contributors with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in AeroPulse spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or stalking;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- sexualized comments, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument;
- personal or political attacks unrelated to the project;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered unprofessional or unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
security-report collaboration, project chat/community spaces, and situations where
someone is officially representing AeroPulse.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Prefer a private communication channel published by the repository maintainer or on
the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public issue. If no private contact
path is available, open a minimal issue requesting a private maintainer contact and
omit names, evidence, and sensitive details until a private channel is established.

Reports should be handled as confidentially as practical and reviewed fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action, including:

1. clarification or correction;
2. a private warning;
3. temporary participation restrictions;
4. removal of comments/contributions;
5. permanent exclusion from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions should consider impact, pattern, intent, remediation, and the
safety of affected participants.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific policy informed by established open-source community
practices, including Contributor Covenant 3.0. It is not a verbatim adoption of that
text.
